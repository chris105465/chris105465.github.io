Order Forms

Below you can find the different forms for my commissions, which can be found on my comm thread. This is something new that I am trying to make things a bit simpler for everyone. Many fields will have an option to leave that up to me instead if you so choose, and I might ask follow up questions just so I can understand what kind of vibe you have in mind. It's important to me that I be on the same page as you so that I can better ensure you will be happy with the final outcome.

Need to Know

  1. The Off-Limits section on the main thread is still a required read before ordering. I might not be the right coder for you and I don't want to waste your time.
  2. Any field outlined in pink is required.
  3. If you'd like to leave an option up to me, then just put "cc" for coder's choice (or Chris' choice lol).
  4. Once you are done, you will be given the completed form that you can paste either into a reply on the thread or into a PM with me.
  5. All communications regarding any of my work will always take place on LD itself.
  6. These forms don't send any information anywhere nor store anything. It's all client-side, so be careful not to reload the page as you may lose your progress.

While my goal was to make the forms as straightforward to fill out as possible, that does not mean that they will always be quick to fill out. Outside of Coder's Choice, if you have a very strong image in mind for what you want, then this is something that you should take your time with as it's something that you are paying for and will likely have on your page for a while, and CSS is not a simple thing. Even my simplest layouts use over 1000 lines of code. The more time you take to make sure that you're happy with your choices, the more likely that you will be happy with the outcome and that's what I want to deliver. I am very proud of my craft, and I always want to make sure I'm able to do my best work for any of my clients.

As always, if you have any questions about font recommendations or anything else that is not answered by this page or my thread, do not hesitate to reach out either on my thread or in PMs.
